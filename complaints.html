Complaints Procedure

Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd

It is our aim to always exceed your expectations, and we hope you never find cause for dissatisfaction during your time with us. However, should you find yourself unhappy with an aspect of our service, we encourage you to let us know and give us the opportunity to put it right.

This policy outlines our complaints procedure: the type of complaints it covers, timescales for addressing, investigating and responding, the process you should expect us to follow, and where to go should we not be able to resolve your complaint, or should you not find it satisfactorily resolved.

This complaints procedure is aimed at resolving complaints quickly and satisfactorily, and further improving the quality of our service.

What Our Complaints Procedure Covers

Our complaints procedure covers complaints you may wish to make with regard to the services we have provided to you, including:

  • Manual errors or mistakes
  • Unexplained delays
  • Staff behaviour / conduct
  • Unpaid refunds
  • Breach of contract / terms of business

How to Make a Complaint

We recognise that complaining about a service, or a person you've been communicating with, doesn't always feel nice. Our priority is that you feel comfortable making a complaint and bringing our attention to anything that has fallen short of your expectations.

If you are unhappy with the service we've provided for any of the above reasons, you can make a complaint however you feel comfortable — speak to any manager across the firm, or express your dissatisfaction in writing by post or email, addressed to the Department Manager or the Complaints Manager.

Whichever way you make your complaint, we will acknowledge receipt via email and/or in writing within 5 working days. We will explain what investigation will take place and give you an estimated timescale, so you know when to expect an outcome. We will also confirm how you wish to be contacted, should we need additional information from you to support our investigation.

If you are making a complaint, we recommend having the following information to hand, as this will allow it to be handled more quickly:

  • Your name and reference number
  • Contact details — including preferred time and method of contact
  • Nature of your complaint, including dates / timescales involved
  • Names of any employees dealing with / involved in your complaint
  • Any relevant additional information

How We Will Action Your Complaint

Our employees are trained to handle your complaint sensitively and efficiently. We will listen to you and seek to understand your experience in detail, asking questions and ensuring we have the full picture before taking action or providing a response or resolution.

We may need to investigate further, which might mean speaking with the employees involved, checking system notes and current process, or listening to call recordings before we can action the complaint.

We aim to have your complaint resolved to your satisfaction at the earliest opportunity, and within 30 days of receiving it. In any event, we will respond to your complaint in full within 8 weeks, in line with guidance from the Financial Conduct Authority and the Financial Ombudsman Service.

Should the Team Leader / Department Manager be unable to resolve your complaint, we will escalate it to our Compliance Department.

Once all information has been considered, you will be contacted by letter or email to confirm whether the company accepts, completely or to some degree, your complaint, and to explain our findings from the investigation. Where applicable, you will be advised what steps we will take to put things right and prevent the same problem recurring.

Where more detailed investigation is required, the process may take longer than 4 working weeks; in any event, we will respond to your complaint in full within 8 weeks.

Financial Ombudsman Service
