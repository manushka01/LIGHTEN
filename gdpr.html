GDPR Policy

How Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd meets its data protection obligations.

Scope Responsibilities Data storage Data breach Subject access requests

Introduction

UK GDPR governs how organisations collect and use personal data, alongside the Data Protection Act 2018.

Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd needs to gather and use certain information about individuals. These can include customers, suppliers, business contacts, employees and other people the organisation has a relationship with or may need to contact.

Why This Policy Exists

  • Comply with the law and follow good practice
  • Protect the rights of staff, customers and partners
  • Be open about how we store and process individuals' data
  • Protect the business from the risk of a data breach

The Key Principles

  • Be processed fairly and lawfully
  • Be obtained only for specific, lawful purposes
  • Be adequate, relevant and not excessive
  • Be accurate and kept up to date
  • Not be held for any longer than necessary
  • Be processed in accordance with the rights of data subjects

Policy Scope

This policy applies to:

  • The head office of Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd
  • All staff and volunteers of Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd
  • All contractors, suppliers and other people working on behalf of Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd

It applies to all data that the company holds relating to identifiable individuals, which can include: names, postal addresses, email addresses and telephone numbers.

Risks

This policy helps protect Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd from real data security risks, including:

  • Breaches of confidentiality — for instance, information being given out inappropriately;
  • Failing to offer choice — individuals should be free to choose how the company uses data relating to them;
  • Reputational damage — for instance, if hackers successfully gained access to sensitive data.

Responsibilities

Everyone who works for or with Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd is responsible for ensuring data is collected, stored and handled appropriately. Each team that handles personal data must ensure it is processed in line with this policy and the key principles.

The Compliance Manager is responsible for:

  • Keeping the board updated about data protection responsibilities, risks and issues;
  • Reviewing all policies and procedures;
  • Arranging training and advice for the people covered by this policy;
  • Handling data protection questions from staff and anyone else covered by this policy;
  • Dealing with subject access requests;
  • Responding to requests for rectification and erasure.

The IT Director is responsible for:

  • Ensuring all systems, services and equipment used for storing data meet acceptable security standards;
  • Performing regular checks and scans to ensure security hardware and software is functioning properly;
  • Evaluating any third-party services the company is considering using to store or process data, such as cloud computing services;
  • Working with other staff, where necessary, to ensure marketing initiatives abide by data protection principles.

General staff guidelines

  • Only people who need data for their work should access it;
  • Data should not be shared informally — employees can request access from their line manager;
  • Lighten will provide training to help staff understand their responsibilities when handling data;
  • Strong passwords must be used and never shared;
  • Personal data should not be disclosed to unauthorised people, within the company or externally;
  • Data should be regularly reviewed, updated if out of date, and deleted and disposed of if no longer required;
  • Employees should ask their line manager or the Compliance Manager if unsure about any aspect of GDPR.

Data Storage

These rules describe how and where data should be safely stored. Questions about storing data safely can be directed to the IT Director.

  • Paper records should be kept in a secure place where unauthorised people cannot see them;
  • When not required, paper or files should be kept in a locked drawer or filing cabinet;
  • Printouts should not be left where unauthorised people could see them, such as on a printer;
  • Printouts should be shredded and disposed of securely when no longer required;
  • Electronic data must be protected from unauthorised access, accidental deletion and malicious hacking attempts;
  • Data should be protected by strong passwords, changed regularly and never shared between employees;
  • Removable media (CDs, DVDs, USB drives) should be kept locked away securely when not in use;
  • Data should only be stored on designated drives and servers, and only uploaded to an approved cloud service;
  • Servers containing personal data should be sited in a secure location, away from general office space;
  • Data should be backed up frequently, and backups tested regularly in line with our standard procedures;
  • Data should never be saved directly to laptops or other mobile devices such as tablets or smartphones;
  • All servers and computers containing data should be protected by approved security software and a firewall.

Data Use

Personal data is of no value to Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd unless the business can make use of it. However, it is when personal data is accessed and used that it is at greatest risk of loss, corruption or theft:

  • Screens should always be locked when left unattended;
  • Personal data should not be shared informally, and never sent by email, as this is not a secure form of communication;
  • Data must be encrypted before being transferred electronically — the IT Director can explain how to send data to authorised external contacts;
  • Personal data should never be transferred outside of the UK or European Economic Area without appropriate safeguards;
  • Employees should not save copies of personal data to their own computers — always access and update the central copy.

Data Breach

Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd is required to notify the ICO of a data breach within 72 hours of it occurring.

  • Staff are aware they should notify the Compliance Manager of any data breaches;
  • A process is in place to make individuals affected aware, should a data breach occur;
  • All possible precautions are taken to ensure data breaches don't happen;
  • The Compliance Manager will notify the ICO of a data breach.

Data Accuracy

The law requires Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd to take reasonable steps to ensure data is accurate and kept up to date. The more important it is that the personal data is accurate, the greater the effort we put into ensuring its accuracy.

  • Data will be held in as few places as necessary — staff should not create unnecessary additional data sets;
  • Staff should take every opportunity to ensure data is updated, for instance by confirming a customer's details when they call;
  • We will make it easy for data subjects to update their information;
  • Data should be updated as inaccuracies are discovered — for instance, if a customer can no longer be reached on a stored telephone number, it should be removed from the database.

Subject Access Requests

All individuals who are the subject of personal data held by Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd are entitled to:

  • Ask what information the company holds about them and why;
  • Ask how to gain access to it;
  • Be informed how to keep it up to date;
  • Be informed how the company is meeting its data obligations.

Subject access requests should be made by email, addressed to the Compliance Manager. The Compliance Manager can supply a standard request form, although individuals do not have to use it, and will aim to provide the relevant data within one month. The identity of anyone making a request will always be verified before any information is provided.

Rectification & Erasure

All individuals who are the subject of personal data held by Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd are entitled to request rectification or erasure of the data held about them. Requests should be made by email to the Compliance Manager, and we will aim to respond within one month.

Special Category Data

There will be some occasions when Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd staff need to obtain and process special category data relating to previous or current health conditions. Staff will gain explicit consent from the individual, who will be made aware of where the information will be held and for what purpose.

Disclosing Data for Other Reasons

In certain circumstances, personal data can be disclosed to law enforcement agencies without the consent of the data subject. Under these circumstances, we will disclose requested data, but the data controller will ensure the request is legitimate, seeking assistance from the board and our Compliance Manager where needed.

Providing Information

Lighten Financial Intermediaries Ltd aims to ensure individuals are aware their data is being processed, and understand how it is used and how to exercise their rights. To that end, our Privacy Policy, visible on the Lighten website, sets out how data relating to individuals is used by the company.
